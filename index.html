A sensible scope

Useful automation, without handing over the keys.

AI systems can make mistakes, and connecting them to business tools creates real security and privacy considerations. We take a deliberately bounded approach.

  • Agree the task, inputs, outputs and limits before work begins.
  • Use the minimum access and permissions needed for the agreed workflow.
  • Prefer draft, review and approval steps for external messages or consequential actions.
  • Explain which services and integrations the workflow relies on.
  • Test with sample or appropriately redacted information before live use.
  • Provide a handover so you understand how to use and pause the workflow.
A clear privacy point: “AI” does not automatically mean private or local. Depending on the configuration, information may be processed by model providers, hosting services or connected applications. We explain the proposed data flow before setup; we do not promise that data never leaves your systems.

Questions, answered

Before you get started.

Do I need to know anything about AI?

No. You need to understand the business task you want to improve. We will explain the proposed workflow in ordinary business terms and identify any decisions or access we need from you.

Do I need to replace my existing software?

Usually not. The intention is to work with tools you already use where suitable integrations are available. We will check compatibility first; if a particular connection is not practical, we will say so rather than imply it is included.

Will the AI send emails or make changes by itself?

Not by default. For external communications or consequential actions, we recommend a review-and-approval step. Any more autonomous behaviour would need to be explicitly scoped, assessed and authorised.

Is my business information kept private?

Privacy depends on the hosting arrangement, AI model, connected services and the information processed. Some configurations send prompts or data to third-party providers. Before setup, we explain the intended data flow and discuss whether it is appropriate for your information. We do not make blanket claims that all processing is local or that no third party can receive data.

Are subscriptions or usage fees included?

No. The project fee covers the work described in the selected package. Third-party software, hosting, model/API usage and other provider charges are separate. We will identify known additional costs before proceeding.

What if my task turns out to be more complicated?

We will explain the issue and agree any revised scope and price before doing additional work. You are not committed to an open-ended project.

What technology do you use?

We select the implementation approach according to the task, required integrations and operating constraints. Open-source agent tools may form part of the underlying setup, but the service is centred on the workflow and its suitability—not on asking you to buy a particular technology.
